Hello!

I'm RedtailWorks — I'm an Oregon-based producer converting ADHD into audio using a mix of digital tools for your listening pleasure.

    My journey into music started around 2011 at age 14, right when I was finding my footing in the furry fandom and discovering LapFox Trax. Dubstep was everywhere and the music I was finding in the community hit different, so I got my hands on a (totaly legal) copy of FL Studio and never looked back.


    Describing my sound is something I genuinely can't do. I experiment across genres constantly and try to make sure no two albums sound the same, except maybe the seasonal stuff. What I make now has a lot more layering and genre blending than my early work did. I commission all my album art too, and rather than dictating what I want, I let the artist listen to the music first and create whatever it inspires in them. RedtailWorks isn't collaborative in the traditional sense, but the people around me, friends, other producers, and artists, shape it more than they probably realize.


    Outside of music I'm into photography, writing, and apparently rebuilding my website on a regular basis. I have ADHD and jump between interests fast, which is honestly why music has stuck. I make it for fun, when I feel creative, with no pressure to be consistent. Most releases will be free. I'm not big on social media, so if you want to keep up, your best bet is right here, my Bandcamp, or YouTube at @RedtailWorks.
